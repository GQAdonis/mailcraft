- **Ask your mail, on your own terms.** Name a model server in File › Options (one on this computer, such as Apple's on-device model served locally, or a provider elsewhere for the accounts you allow) and you can narrow the message list in plain words, seeing and accepting the search it becomes first, and ask questions about your mail, with a streamed answer that cites the messages it came from. Nothing is sent to any model until you set one up; agents can search your mail through MCP (`search_mail`) but cannot ask your model anything. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md#ai-model-servers).
